# Kandra Glossary

Status: derivative reference.

## Authority

Permission for a claim, design, implementation, or repository transition to be treated as valid within its owning process. Evidence can support authority but does not automatically create it.

## Controlling source

The most specific current normative repository source that owns the behavior or decision.

## Supporting context

Useful information that helps explain a decision but cannot override the controlling source.

## E1 research context

Kandra label for a returned Deep Research report before consequential claims are promoted. E1 is research input, not runtime proof or implementation authority.

## Preserved input

Original research/report content retained unchanged so later reviewers can audit what was actually supplied or returned.

## Clean derivative / clean intake

A separate, explicitly labelled derivative that reconciles citations, claims, contradictions, and evidence states without rewriting the preserved original.

## Source register

Durable inventory of sources with stable IDs, exact locators, version/revision/date context, evidence lane, scoped support, and limitations.

## Claim register

Durable inventory of material claims with stable IDs, evidence state, supporting sources, consequences, limitations, and missing proof.

## Evidence lane

Category of evidence distinguished by what it can prove: public research, decompilation/static, repository/static, host/compiled, Editor/UI, operational/runtime, persistence/save, and so on.

## L0-L4

FOA-SDK validation layers:

- L0 repository/static;
- L1 unit/contract;
- L2 configure/build/compiled host;
- L3 Editor/UI/manual host interaction;
- L4 operational/runtime.

## RH0-RH5

Owner-level Kandra review ladder:

- RH0 intake;
- RH1 claim evaluation;
- RH2 domain/repository review;
- RH3 independent review;
- RH4 validation/evidence review;
- RH5 human promotion.

## R0-R5

A repository research-gate model used by at least one FOA-SDK research topic:

- R0 intake integrity;
- R1 durable sourcing;
- R2 repository reconciliation;
- R3 authority-lane classification;
- R4 experiment readiness;
- R5 promotion candidate.

R0-R5 and RH0-RH5 are related but not interchangeable.

## Promotion candidate

Research conclusion ready to be considered by the normative owner. It is not yet an accepted decision.

## Human promotion

Explicit acceptance by the human promotion owner into a normative project source. Agents may prepare evidence and recommendations but cannot be final promotion owner.

## Normative destination

Architecture, design, schema, gate, durable decision, current task, or other owning source that can actually authorise implementation.

## Exact-head evidence

Validation bound to the exact commit/ref being reviewed rather than a stale, nearby, or later source state.

## Protected material

Secrets, credentials, private paths/data, saves, signing material, proprietary commercial content, installations, or other inputs governed by the protected-files policy.

## Fail closed

Stop the affected operation when required authority, evidence, permission, method, or tooling is unavailable rather than guessing or substituting a different operation.

## Runtime proof

Evidence from the actual runtime/operational layer needed by the claim. Static research, decompilation, build success, or Editor behavior are not runtime proof.

## "Next researched task"

A recommendation or future-work pointer in a report. It has no execution authority by itself.

## Truth over completion

The governing discipline that an incomplete but accurate state is preferable to a completed-looking claim unsupported by performed and verified evidence.
